A little context

Research, and life outside it.

I’m a third-year Data Science and Engineering undergraduate at MIT Manipal, graduating in 2028.

My focus is AI safety. I approach it through behavioral explainability and LLM evaluation, studying reasoning faithfulness, confidence calibration, and red teaming to uncover model failures.

I’m also interested in how reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) shapes model behavior, and in instrumental convergence: why systems with different goals may develop similar incentives to seek power or preserve themselves.

Outside research, I play keyboard, listen to Bollywood classics, and enjoy coffee and the sound of rain.
